STUDIO GUIDE

How Stemit works

  1. Choose an audio file from your device.
  2. Run the available stereo layer extraction.
  3. Adjust each layer's level, pan, mute or solo.
  4. Export the mix or download individual WAV layers.

AI stem separation, tempo, pitch, looping and metronome tools are not active yet.
