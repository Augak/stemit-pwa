STUDIO GUIDE

How Stemit works

  1. Choose an audio file from your device.
  2. Run the available stereo layer extraction.
  3. Adjust each layer's volume, pan, mute or solo.
  4. Export the mix or download individual WAV layers.

Four- and six-stem separation are shown as planned modes and are not active until their neural model is integrated.
