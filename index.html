QUICK GUIDE

Getting started

  1. Choose an audio file from your phone or computer.
  2. Select Vocal focus or Instrumental focus.
  3. Press Process audio, listen to the result, and download it as WAV.

This prototype extracts the stereo centre or sides. It is not the full AI vocal separation engine, and it works best with stereo music.
